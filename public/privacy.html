Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This portfolio is operated by Zaira Christa. Privacy questions can be sent to hello@zairachrista.com.

Information collected

If you use the contact form, the site processes the name, email address, company (if supplied), and message you submit. Basic technical information, such as an IP address and request metadata, may be processed temporarily to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

Contact details are used only to read and respond to enquiries. Technical information is used to prevent abuse, maintain security, and diagnose service problems. The legal basis is the operator's legitimate interest in responding to professional enquiries and protecting the website; where applicable, pre-contractual steps may also apply.

Sharing and processors

Information is not sold. It may be processed by the website host and, when the contact form is enabled, the configured email delivery provider (Resend). These providers act only as needed to host the site or deliver the enquiry. Their locations and safeguards should be reviewed before launch.

Retention

Enquiries are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond and keep appropriate professional records, then deleted. Security rate-limit identifiers are one-way hashed, held in memory, and expire after approximately 15 minutes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact Zaira using the email address above to make a request.

Cookies

This website does not currently enable analytics or advertising cookies. A consent preference is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember your choice. See the cookie policy for details.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website's features or service providers change. The date above identifies the latest version.
