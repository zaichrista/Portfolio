BRAND STRATEGY 01

AËSOP

From product to philosophy.

How Aesop built a global brand through a different kind of luxury — one rooted in intellect, design and a deeper sense of human care.

BRAND STRATEGY / CULTURE / RETAIL EXPERIENCE / 2023

01

THE CONTEXT

In a beauty industry defined by excess, Aesop emerged with a quieter proposition. Founded in 1987 in Melbourne, the brand set out to create high-quality products with considered ingredients, thoughtful design, and a deeper respect for people and place. This wasn’t just skincare — it was a different way of thinking about care.

“Aesop doesn’t sell skincare. It sells a state of mind.”

02

THE INSIGHT

People weren’t just looking for better products — they were looking for a more meaningful relationship with them. Aesop understood that modern consumers crave slowness, substance and spaces that feel considered.

03

THE STRATEGY

Aesop built a holistic brand world where every touchpoint reinforces the same philosophy: restraint, rigour and respect. This comes to life through:

  1. Distinctive, timeless design
  2. Experiential retail environments
  3. Thoughtful storytelling
  4. A consistent global language

04

THE OUTCOME

Aesop has become a globally recognised brand with a devoted following, 300+ stores worldwide, and a reputation for doing things differently — proving that a brand can be both commercially successful and culturally meaningful.
