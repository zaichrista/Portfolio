I hold a BA in Music from Oxford and hold an MA in Cultural and Creative Industries from King’s College London, where my research sits at the intersection of nightlife, sound, and identity. I’ve designed runway shows, built brand worlds from scratch, developed theoretical concepts in my academic writing, and directed photoshoots. The thread connecting all of it is the same obsession: why do certain spaces, sounds, and objects make people feel like themselves?
