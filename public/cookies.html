Cookie policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This portfolio does not currently enable advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. It stores your consent preference in your browser’s local storage so that your choice can be remembered. This preference stays on your device and is not used to identify you.

If this changes

Optional analytics, embedded media, or other non-essential technology must not load until the visitor has made an informed choice where consent is legally required. This policy and the privacy policy must be updated before those services are enabled, and visitors must be able to withdraw consent as easily as they gave it.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you inspect, block, and delete cookies in their privacy settings. Blocking essential storage can affect sites that require it, although this portfolio currently does not.
